### Best eight, one-word tightening
- borborygmus — 11: keep the exact stomach-rumble word; its sound does the explaining.
- blunderbuss — 11: keep the clumsy, comic cannon in one compact noun.
- bumbershoot — 11: keep the surprising umbrella-name, no gloss.
- widdershins — 11: keep the strange directional turn, no extra setup.
- squinch — 7: keep the pinched, visual little action.
- absquatulate — 12: keep the oddball exit verb, no sentence around it.
- collywobbles — 12: keep the whole wobbling feeling in its sound.
- flapdoodle — 10: keep the nonsense itself, not an explanation of nonsense.

Each final form is one word, so there is no removable filler; the strongest sound/meaning turn is the last thing the reader gets.
